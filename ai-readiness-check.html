Before you enrol

  • Most lessons in AI for Essential Digital Skills (AI4EDS) open only after you enrol. Two Module 1 lessons are free previews.
  • The course is free and self-paced, with five modules, a quiz per module and practical exercises.
  • Total hours, deadlines, language and certificate details are not stated on the course page. Check these before you commit time.
  • Eligibility for the AVPN-funded programme is not clear from the course page. Check the terms if it matters to you.
  • AI tools change quickly. Treat tool-specific steps as examples and check the current interface.

Other routes on the site

If you want to build AI-powered apps, look at Vibe Coding 101. If your focus is governance and ethics, look at Responsible AI.
